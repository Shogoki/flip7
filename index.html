      Scoring cheat sheet

      • Score = sum of your unique number cards (0–12).
      • ×2 doubles that sum, then +2…+10 modifiers are added on top.
      • Collect 7 different numbers → Flip 7! +15 bonus and the round ends.
      • Draw a duplicate number → bust, score 0 for the round (a Second Chance discards the duplicate instead).
      • First player to reach the target score (200 by default) wins.
